Scores respond over time to the payment and balance behavior recorded in the underlying file. Paying every obligation as agreed, keeping revolving balances

Be cautious of any organization that offers, for a fee, to create a new credit identity, to remove accurate information, or to guarantee a specific score increase.

Section 16. Inquiries and Rate Shopping
An inquiry recorded when a creditor obtains your report in connection with an application may affect a score modestly. Scoring models used for mortgage
lending generally treat multiple mortgage inquiries occurring within a defined shopping window as a single inquiry, so comparing offers among several lenders
within a short period does not compound the effect.

A request you make for your own report, and a promotional or account-review inquiry made without your application, are not treated as inquiries by scoring
models. Comparison shopping is therefore not penalized in the way consumers frequently fear, and it remains the most effective means of evaluating the cost
of credit.

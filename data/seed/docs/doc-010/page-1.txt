Nothing in this document changes the interest rate, payment amount, or maturity date of your loan. This informational notice is furnished to John Doe at
the origination of loan number 1234567890 to explain, in advance, the circumstances under which ABC Mortgage Company or a subsequent servicer
may obtain hazard insurance on the property located at 456 XYZ Avenue, XYZ City, NY 12345 at your expense, and the notices you would receive

coverage to lapse at any point during the life of the loan.

(a) Your Duty to Insure the Property
The security instrument you sign at closing requires you to keep the improvements on the property insured against loss by fire, hazards included within the
term "extended coverage," and any other hazards for which insurance is required, in the amounts and for the periods that the lender requires. The carrier you
select must be acceptable, and the policy must name the lender and its successors and assigns as mortgagee or loss payee so that the lender receives notice
of cancellation and is protected in the event of loss.

You choose your own insurance provider, and nothing in this notice restricts that choice. The coverage must remain continuously in force for the entire term of
the loan, and a policy that is cancelled, not renewed, or reduced below the required amount does not satisfy the obligation even if the lapse is brief or
inadvertent. You should also furnish evidence of coverage to the servicer at each renewal.

(b) Applicable Law and Compliance
This document and the transaction it concerns are subject to the federal statutes and regulations governing residential mortgage credit and to the law of the
State of NY. Where federal law preempts or supplements state law as to a particular matter, federal law controls as to that matter; where state law affords you a
greater protection that is not preempted, that protection applies.

If a change in law or regulation requires a provision of this document to be modified, the provision is deemed amended to the minimum extent necessary to
bring it into compliance, and the remainder continues in effect. No provision should be construed to require any act that applicable law prohibits, or to prohibit
any act that applicable law requires.

(c) About Lender-Placed Coverage
Lender-placed insurance, also called force-placed or creditor-placed insurance, is a policy that a servicer purchases when it has a reasonable basis to believe
that required coverage on a mortgaged property is not in force. The policy protects the lender's interest in the improvements. It is typically written on a blanket
or master program, is more expensive than a policy you would obtain yourself, and its cost is charged to your account.

Lender-placed coverage is materially narrower than a standard homeowners policy. It generally does not insure your personal property, does not provide
liability coverage, does not pay for additional living expenses if the dwelling becomes uninhabitable, and may insure only the outstanding balance of the loan
rather than the full replacement cost of the structure. For those reasons it is never an economical substitute for coverage you obtain directly.

(d) Telephone Communications and Call Monitoring
Telephone calls between you and ABC Mortgage Company may be monitored or recorded for quality assurance, training, and recordkeeping purposes, and
you consent to that monitoring and recording by continuing with a call after being advised of it. Recordings are retained in accordance with our records
schedule and are used only for the purposes described here and as applicable law otherwise permits or requires.

By providing a telephone number in connection with this transaction, you agree that we, our affiliates, our agents, and our service providers may contact you at
that number regarding the account, including by using an automatic dialing system or a prerecorded or artificial voice, and by text message, to the extent
permitted by applicable law. Message and data rates charged by your carrier may apply to any text message you receive.

You may withdraw consent to be contacted at a particular number, or ask that we use a different number or another method entirely, by notifying us at (555)
555-0120 or in writing at 100 ABC Plaza, XYZ City, NY 12345. Withdrawing consent for automated calls does not prevent us from delivering the notices that
law requires, which may still be sent by mail or by another permitted method.

(e) Advance Notices
A servicer may not charge you for lender-placed insurance unless it has first sent a written notice at least forty-five (45) days before assessing any premium.
That notice states that the servicer lacks evidence of required coverage, identifies the coverage the servicer requires, states that lender-placed insurance may
be more expensive and narrower than a policy you obtain yourself, and requests that you provide evidence of your existing coverage.

A second, reminder notice is sent no earlier than thirty (30) days after the first and at least fifteen (15) days before any charge is assessed. If you provide
evidence of coverage at any point before the placement, no lender-placed policy is purchased. The notices are mailed to your address of record, which is
another reason to keep your contact information current with the servicer.

(g) Termination of Placed Coverage
If a lender-placed policy has been purchased and you later provide evidence that acceptable coverage was in force, the servicer must cancel the placed policy
within fifteen (15) days of receiving that evidence, refund all premiums and related fees charged for any period of overlapping coverage, and remove the
corresponding charge from your account. A corrected account statement reflecting the adjustment is then issued.

Where coverage genuinely lapsed for a period, the placed policy remains in force for that gap and the premium attributable to it remains payable. Obtaining
your own policy promptly is therefore the most effective way to limit the cost. The servicer will not continue placed coverage beyond the point at which
acceptable evidence of your own insurance takes effect.

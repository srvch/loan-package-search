(r) Coverage Amounts and Valuation
A placed policy is typically written for an amount tied to the last known coverage on the property, to the replacement cost estimated by the placement carrier, or
to the outstanding principal balance, subject to the requirements of applicable law. That amount protects the lender's interest and may be substantially less
than the amount required to rebuild the dwelling.

If a loss occurred while placed coverage was in effect, proceeds would be applied in accordance with the security instrument and would ordinarily be
insufficient to restore the property fully, and no proceeds would be available for personal property, liability claims, or living expenses. The gap between placed
coverage and a standard homeowners policy is the practical reason to maintain your own.

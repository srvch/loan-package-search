1) Prohibited Bases of Discrimination
The Equal Credit Opportunity Act and Regulation B make it unlawful for any creditor, including ABC Mortgage Company, to discriminate against an applicant
with respect to any aspect of a credit transaction on the basis of race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, or age, provided the applicant has the
capacity to enter into a binding contract. These prohibited bases extend to every phase of the credit relationship, including the taking and evaluation of
applications, the establishment of terms and conditions, the administration of accounts, and the collection of amounts owed. ABC Mortgage Company does not
consider any prohibited basis as a negative factor in its assessment of the creditworthiness of John Doe.

It is further unlawful for a creditor to discriminate against an applicant because all or part of the applicant's income derives from any public assistance program,
or because the applicant has in good faith exercised any right under the Consumer Credit Protection Act. The federal agencies that enforce compliance with
this law may, in appropriate circumstances, consider a creditor's conduct toward applicants who exercise such rights. Accordingly, ABC Mortgage Company
evaluates income from public assistance and other lawful sources on the same terms applied to comparable income, and it does not penalize John Doe for
asserting any statutory right.

These protections apply uniformly regardless of the type, size, or purpose of the credit requested, whether the extension of credit is secured or unsecured, and
whether the transaction is consummated, withdrawn, or denied. ABC Mortgage Company maintains internal policies, training, and monitoring designed to
ensure that personnel involved in the origination, underwriting, and servicing of credit adhere to these requirements at every stage of the transaction
associated with loan number 1234567890.

the loan documents.

This document binds and benefits you and us and our respective heirs, executors, administrators, successors, and permitted assigns. You may not assign or

release you from liability for the debt unless we expressly agree in writing to a release.

3) Right to a Statement of Reasons
When ABC Mortgage Company takes adverse action on an application for credit, or on an existing account, you have the right under the Equal Credit
Opportunity Act to a statement of the specific reasons for that action. Adverse action includes a denial or revocation of credit, a change in the terms of an
existing account that is not favorable to John Doe and that is not applied to all or substantially all of a class of the creditor's accounts, and a refusal to grant
credit in substantially the amount or on substantially the terms requested in an application.

The statement of specific reasons will either be provided automatically with the notice of adverse action or be made available upon your timely request. If the
reasons are not disclosed in the initial notice, ABC Mortgage Company will include a statement of your right to request the reasons within sixty (60) days of the
notification, together with the name, address, and telephone number of the person or office from which the reasons may be obtained. Upon a timely oral or
written request, ABC Mortgage Company will furnish the specific reasons in writing within a reasonable period of time, generally not exceeding thirty (30) days.

The reasons disclosed will be specific and will indicate the principal factors that influenced the action taken; statements that the action was based on internal
standards or policies, or that the applicant failed to achieve a qualifying score, are not by themselves sufficient. Any statement of reasons furnished in
connection with loan number 1234567890 will accurately reflect the actual basis upon which the credit decision was made.

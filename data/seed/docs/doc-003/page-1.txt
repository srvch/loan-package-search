PARTIAL PAYMENT AND SUSPENSE ACCOUNT POLICY (DISCLOSURE TO BORROWER)
Loan #1234567890 · ABC Mortgage Company · 11/19/2024

This policy explains how payments received on loan number 1234567890 are applied, what happens when an amount received is less than a full
contractual payment, how funds held in suspense are treated, and how you may direct an additional amount toward principal. The partial payment policy
applicable to your loan is also stated on your Closing Disclosure, and the entry on that document controls in the event of any difference. This statement
is furnished for your convenience so that the treatment of every payment you make on the property at 456 XYZ Avenue, XYZ City, NY 12345 is clear
from the outset. A copy of this document is retained in the file maintained for loan number 1234567890.

1.0 What Constitutes a Full Payment
A full contractual payment consists of the scheduled principal and interest, plus the monthly escrow deposit where an escrow account is maintained, plus any
other recurring amount identified on your periodic statement, such as an optional product premium you have elected. The total appears on your billing
statement as the amount due. Any remittance below that total is a partial payment regardless of how close it comes to the amount due.

Amounts past due from a prior period are added to the current amount due. Where more than one payment is outstanding, the total due reflects each unpaid
installment together with any late charge properly assessed, and a remittance sufficient to cover only one installment is applied to the oldest outstanding
installment first.

2.0 Computation of Time Periods
Unless a particular statute, regulation, or provision states otherwise, a period expressed in days is measured in calendar days, the day of the act or event that
starts the period is excluded, and the last day is included. Where the last day falls on a Saturday, a Sunday, or a legal public holiday, the period runs to the end
of the next day that is not one of those days.

Several requirements applicable to residential mortgage transactions are measured in business days, and the definition of a business day differs among them:
one rule counts days on which our offices are open for substantially all business, while another counts all calendar days except Sundays and legal public
holidays. Where a specific count controls a right or a deadline, it is stated in the provision that establishes it.

Where a period is measured from the delivery of a document, delivery by mail is generally presumed to occur three business days after mailing unless earlier
receipt is documented. Because these conventions determine real deadlines in your transaction, confirm the applicable dates with us or with your settlement
agent rather than estimating them.

3.0 What Happens to a Partial Payment
Where the servicer accepts a partial payment, the funds are ordinarily placed in a suspense or unapplied-funds account rather than applied immediately to the
loan. Funds remain in suspense until enough has accumulated to make a full contractual payment, at which point the full amount is applied in the standard
order and credited as of the date the account reached that total.

Funds held in suspense do not reduce the principal balance and do not stop the accrual of interest. Your periodic statement identifies any amount held in
suspense so that the status of the account is visible to you. If you prefer that a partial remittance be returned rather than held, notify the servicer at (555)
555-0120 at the time the payment is made.

4.0 Communication Preferences
You may tell us how you prefer to be reached, and we will honor a stated preference where the nature of the communication and applicable law permit. Certain
communications must be delivered in a specific manner regardless of preference, and those are sent by the required method even where you have asked to be
contacted only in another way.

Marketing communications are distinct from servicing communications. You may opt out of marketing by contacting us at (555) 555-0120 or by following the
instructions contained in the communication itself, and doing so does not affect the delivery of statements, disclosures, escrow analyses, or any other
communication concerning the administration of loan number 1234567890.

Where you have asked to receive communications electronically, keep the electronic address on file current and add our sending address to the list of
permitted senders in your mail application, so that a notice is not diverted to a junk folder. A notice diverted in that manner remains effective as of the date it
was sent.

5.0 Effect of Accepting a Payment
Acceptance of a partial payment, or of a payment tendered after the due date, does not waive the requirement that each installment be paid in full when due
and does not establish a course of dealing that alters the terms of the note. The servicer may accept a partial payment in one month and decline to accept one
in the next without notice, subject to the disclosed policy and applicable law.

A remittance marked "paid in full," or accompanied by a restrictive endorsement or a condition, may be accepted and processed without being bound by the
notation, and no such notation operates as an accord and satisfaction unless the servicer agrees in a separate writing signed by an authorized officer. If you
intend to satisfy the loan in full, request a payoff statement and remit in accordance with it.

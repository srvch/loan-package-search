13.0 Initial and Annual Statements
The initial escrow account statement is provided at settlement or within forty-five (45) days after the account is established. It itemizes the estimated taxes,
insurance premiums, and other charges expected to be paid from the account during the first twelve months, the anticipated disbursement dates, and the
amount of the cushion.

Thereafter an annual escrow account statement is provided within thirty (30) days after the end of each computation year. It shows the actual activity of the
past year against the projections, the projected activity for the coming year, the new monthly payment, and the reason for any change. Compare each annual
statement to the prior one so that a change in taxes or premiums is understood when it occurs rather than later.

15.0 Escrow at Payoff or Transfer
When the loan is paid in full, any balance remaining in escrow after the final disbursements is refunded to you within the period required by law, and thereafter
you pay taxes and insurance directly. Confirm with the taxing authority and the insurer that future bills are directed to you rather than to the former servicer,
since a bill sent to the wrong party is a common source of a missed payment after a payoff.

When servicing transfers, escrow funds transfer with the loan and the transferee servicer must honor the terms of the existing account, including any payment
arrangement for a shortage. The transferee conducts its own analysis in the ordinary course, and a change in the payment following a transfer reflects that
analysis rather than the transfer itself.

Scope of Work

 The scope of work for this assignment was determined by the intended use of the report — a purchase-money mortgage finance transaction — and the intended user, ABC
 Mortgage Company. The appraiser inspected the interior and exterior of the subject on 11/02/2024, measured the improvements, photographed the subject and each
 comparable from the street, and researched the multiple listing service, county public records and the contract of sale. Comparable sales were selected from the subject
 neighborhood and analyzed on a price-per-unit and paired-sales basis. The sales comparison approach was developed; the cost and income approaches were not necessary
 for credible assignment results and were not developed.

Highest and Best Use

 The subject is a one-unit dwelling on a 0.25 acres site zoned R-1 Single Family Residential. The present use is legal and conforming, is physically possible, financially feasible
 and maximally productive; the highest and best use as improved is therefore the present residential use. No alternative use would produce a greater return to the site.

Comparable Selection and Adjustment Support

 The comparable sales are the most recent closed sales of similar one-unit dwellings located within one-half mile of the subject. All are in the subject neighborhood, are of similar
 site size, design, quality and age, and required no adjustment for location, view, leasehold interest, room count or functional utility. Adjustments were limited to gross living area
 at a market-supported rate, market conditions measured from the date of each sale, and amenity differences. Gross adjustments are within accepted guidelines and the
 adjusted values bracket and support the opinion of value.

Reconciliation of the Adjusted Values

 The three closed sales bracket the subject in gross living area, age and amenities. Each adjusted sale price indicates $620,000.00; the range of adjusted values is nil, gross
 adjustments do not exceed the guideline limits and no single adjustment exceeds ten percent of the unadjusted sale price. The sales comparison approach therefore carries all
 of the weight in the final reconciliation, and the opinion of market value is $620,000.00 as of 11/02/2024.

                       Additional Comments — Scope of Work, Analysis and Conclusions
                        This appraisal report was prepared for ABC Mortgage Company for a purchase transaction secured by the subject property. The scope of work included a complete
                       interior and exterior inspection of the subject on 11/02/2024, an exterior inspection of each comparable sale from the street, and research of the multiple listing service
                       and county public records. The contract of sale, the subject's listing history and the prior transfer history of the subject and each comparable were analyzed. No
                       extraordinary assumptions or hypothetical conditions were made.

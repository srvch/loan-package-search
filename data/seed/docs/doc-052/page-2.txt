10.0 Float-Down Features
Certain programs offer a one-time float-down permitting a locked rate to be adjusted downward if the market improves by a stated margin before a defined
point in the transaction. Where a float-down is available, its cost, the improvement threshold that triggers it, the deadline for exercising it, and the number of
times it may be used are stated in the confirmation.

A float-down must be exercised affirmatively; it does not operate automatically when the market improves. If your confirmation does not identify a float-down
feature, none applies to your lock, and a later improvement in market pricing does not alter the locked terms.

11.0 Effect of a Transfer of Servicing
The servicing of loan number 1234567890 — the collection of payments, the administration of any escrow account, and the handling of your inquiries — may
be transferred to another company at any time. A transfer of servicing is not a sale of your obligation to a different borrower and does not change the interest
rate, the payment amount, the maturity date, or any other term of the note or the security instrument.

Where servicing transfers, you receive a notice from the transferor at least fifteen (15) days before the effective date and a notice from the transferee within
fifteen (15) days after it, unless the transfer occurs in circumstances for which the law permits a combined or later notice. The notices identify both companies,
the date payments should begin going to the new servicer, and the telephone numbers at which each may be reached.

For sixty (60) days after the effective date of a transfer, a payment received by the prior servicer on or before its due date may not be treated as late merely
because it was sent to the wrong company. Even so, redirect your payments promptly, and update any automatic payment arrangement you maintain through
your own financial institution, since that arrangement is under your control rather than ours.

16.0 Points, Credits, and Pricing
A quoted rate is inseparable from the points or credits associated with it. Paying discount points buys a lower rate; accepting a higher rate produces a lender
credit that offsets closing costs. Neither is inherently better, and the right choice depends on how long you expect to hold the loan and on the funds available
for closing.

When comparing offers, compare the rate, the points, and the total of lender charges together, on the same day and for the same lock period, since pricing
moves during the day and a quote taken on Tuesday is not comparable to one taken on Thursday. Your Loan Estimate presents these elements in a
standardized format for exactly that purpose.

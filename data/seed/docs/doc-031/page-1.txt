You may request an additional copy of this document at any time at no charge. This document describes how to request a payoff statement for loan
number 1234567890, what such a statement contains, how long it remains valid, and how funds must be remitted in order to satisfy the debt secured by
the property at 456 XYZ Avenue, XYZ City, NY 12345. A payoff statement is the servicer's official calculation of the total amount required to pay the loan
in full as of a stated date. It is used in connection with a sale, a refinance, or a voluntary prepayment, and it is relied upon by settlement agents and title
insurers. These procedures apply throughout the life of the loan and are provided at origination for your future reference.

Article I. Authorized Requesters
A payoff statement may be requested by you, by a co-borrower obligated on the note, or by a third party you have authorized in writing, such as a settlement
agent, title company, closing attorney, or refinancing lender. A third-party request must be accompanied by a signed borrower authorization identifying the
requesting party and the loan, or the request will be returned without a statement.

Because a payoff statement discloses the outstanding balance and other account information, it is treated as confidential. We do not furnish payoff figures by
telephone to unverified callers, and we do not send statements to an address or electronic destination that is not associated with the account or identified in a
valid authorization. These limitations protect you against payoff fraud, which typically begins with the interception or alteration of a legitimate statement.

Article II. Response Time
An accurate payoff statement is provided within a reasonable time and, in any event, within the period prescribed by applicable federal and NY law, which is
generally not more than seven (7) business days after receipt of a written request, excluding legal public holidays, Saturdays, and Sundays. Where a delay is
caused by a natural disaster, a bankruptcy proceeding, or similar circumstances, the period may be extended to the extent the law allows.

Requests submitted through an established settlement-agent channel are frequently fulfilled more quickly. To avoid pressure at the end of a transaction, we
recommend ordering the statement as soon as a closing date is set rather than in the final days before funding.

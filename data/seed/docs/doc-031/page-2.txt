Article VII. Contents of the Statement
A payoff statement identifies the unpaid principal balance, interest accrued through the payoff date, the per diem interest rate applicable after that date, any
escrow advance or shortage, recording or reconveyance charges, statement or wire fees where permitted, and any other amount properly due under the loan
documents. It states the date through which the quoted figure is good and the exact remittance instructions.

The statement does not reflect payments that have not yet been received or that have not cleared, and it does not anticipate a payment returned for insufficient
funds after the statement date. If a scheduled monthly payment is made after the statement is issued, the payoff amount changes, and the settlement agent
should confirm the figure before disbursing.

Article VIII. Per Diem Interest and Expiration
Interest accrues daily on the unpaid principal balance. If funds are received after the good-through date shown on the statement, additional per diem interest is
due for each day beyond that date, and a short payoff leaves a residual balance that keeps the lien in place. Settlement agents ordinarily include several days
of additional per diem to avoid this outcome.

A statement that has expired should not be relied upon. Request an updated statement whenever the closing is postponed past the good-through date,
whenever a monthly payment is made after the statement is issued, or whenever a charge such as a property inspection or escrow disbursement occurs in the
interim.

Article IX. Remittance Instructions and Wire Fraud
Payoff funds must be remitted exactly as the statement directs, whether by wire transfer, certified funds, or cashier's check. Wiring instructions appear only on
the statement itself. We do not change remittance instructions by email, and any message purporting to convey revised wiring instructions should be treated as
fraudulent until verified by telephone with our office at a number you have independently confirmed.

Funds sent to an incorrect destination are not credited to your account and do not satisfy the debt. Because payoff wire fraud is a persistent risk in residential
transactions, we ask that you and your settlement agent verify instructions verbally before initiating any transfer of funds relating to loan number 1234567890.

Article XIII. Fraud Prevention
Mortgage transactions attract fraud, most commonly in the form of messages that appear to come from a lender, a settlement agent, or a real estate
professional and that direct funds to an account controlled by a criminal. We do not transmit wiring instructions by electronic message, and we do not change
previously issued remittance instructions by electronic message. Verify any such instruction by telephone at a number you have independently confirmed
before transferring funds.

Report a suspicious communication concerning loan number 1234567890 to us at (555) 555-0120 promptly, and preserve the message rather than deleting it.
If funds have already been transmitted, contact your financial institution immediately and ask that a recall be initiated; the prospects of recovery diminish
sharply within hours, and prompt action is by far the most important factor.

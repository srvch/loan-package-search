There are certain everyday business purposes for which federal law does not permit you to limit our sharing of your personal information. These include

report to credit bureaus and other consumer reporting agencies. We also share as necessary to protect against or prevent actual or potential fraud,

In addition, we may disclose your personal information to companies that perform services on our behalf, including providers of document preparation, data

(f) Sharing With Affiliates
Affiliates are companies related to us by common ownership or control, and they can include both financial and nonfinancial companies. We may share with
our affiliates information about your transactions and experiences with us, such as your account history and payment record. This type of information sharing is
permitted by law and is generally not subject to your right to opt out, although we will honor any additional limitations required under applicable state law.

We may also share among our affiliates certain other information about you, including information we obtain from your application and from consumer reporting
agencies relating to your creditworthiness. Federal law gives you the right to limit the sharing of this category of information so that our affiliates may not use it
to market their products and services to you. If you wish to limit such sharing, you may do so by following the instructions set out in the section of this notice
describing your choices and how to contact us.

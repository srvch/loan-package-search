(a) Purpose of This Notice
Financial companies choose how they share your personal information, and federal law, including the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act and its implementing
regulations, requires us to tell you how we collect, share, and protect that information. We are providing this notice so that you understand what we do and the
choices you may have. The types of personal information we collect and share depend on the product or service you have with us and on the nature of our
relationship. We furnish this notice when you become a customer and, as required by applicable law, on a periodic basis for so long as you maintain a
continuing relationship with ABC Mortgage Company.

When you are no longer our customer, we continue to share your information as described in this notice, subject to the limitations imposed by law. Nothing in
this notice is intended to expand or restrict any rights you may have under other agreements between you and ABC Mortgage Company or under applicable
federal and state law. If state law provides you with additional rights or protections, we will honor those rights to the extent they apply to your account and to the
information we maintain about you.

(b) Events Beyond Our Control
Neither party is liable for a failure or delay in performing an obligation under this document to the extent the failure or delay results from a cause beyond that
party's reasonable control, including an act of God, a natural disaster, a fire, an epidemic, an act of war or terrorism, a civil disturbance, a labor dispute, a
failure of telecommunications or utility service, or an action of a governmental authority.

This provision does not excuse the obligation to pay money when due, and it does not extend a deadline established by statute or regulation except to the
extent that authority itself provides relief. Where a widespread disruption affects a community, relief programs are frequently announced by regulators or by
loan program sponsors, and we administer any such program according to its terms.

Where an event of this character affects our ability to answer telephones, process mail, or deliver documents on the ordinary schedule, we will restore normal
service as promptly as practicable and will handle matters delayed by the disruption without penalty to you where the delay was not attributable to you.

(c) Categories of Information Collected
We collect personal information about you in connection with providing you a financial product or service. The information we collect depends on the product or
service you request and may include your name, address, and telephone number; your Social Security number and date of birth; your income, employment
information, and assets; and your account balances, payment history, and transaction or loss history. We obtain much of this information directly from you on
applications and other forms, and through your communications with us by telephone, in writing, or electronically.

We also collect your personal information from others, such as credit bureaus, affiliates, and other companies. For example, we may receive your credit history,
creditworthiness, and credit scores from consumer reporting agencies, and we may receive verification of employment, deposits, and other account information
from third parties you authorize. We maintain records relating to the origination, servicing, and, where applicable, the transfer of your loan, including documents
you sign at closing and information generated during the life of your account.

The categories of information we collect and the manner in which we use that information may change over time as products, services, and legal requirements
evolve. Where required, we will update this notice to reflect material changes in our information practices. We collect only the personal information reasonably
necessary to provide the products and services you request, to administer your account, and to comply with our legal and regulatory obligations.

All financial companies need to share customers' personal information to run their everyday business. In the section that follows, we describe the reasons
financial companies can share their customers' personal information, the reasons ABC Mortgage Company chooses to share, and whether you can limit that
sharing. Whether you can limit sharing depends on the reason for which the information is shared and on the protections afforded to you by federal and state
law.

except where federal or state law provides otherwise.

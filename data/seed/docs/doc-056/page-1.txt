Scope of Your Consent
Your consent applies to all records and communications that we, our affiliates, our service providers, and our successors and assigns are required by law to
provide or make available to you in writing, as well as to any other records and communications we elect to provide electronically in connection with your loan,
your application, and any related products or services. This includes, without limitation, application disclosures, loan estimates, closing disclosures, account
statements, periodic notices, privacy notices, tax and insurance communications, change-in-terms notices, and any amendments or supplements to the
foregoing. Collectively, these are referred to in this Consent as "Communications."

By consenting, you agree that we may satisfy any legal obligation to deliver a Communication to you in writing by delivering that Communication electronically.
Electronic delivery may occur by transmitting the Communication to the email address you have provided, by posting the Communication to a secure website
or online portal and notifying you that it is available, by displaying or delivering it through a mobile application, or by another electronic method reasonably
designed to provide the Communication to you. Your consent remains effective for the duration of your relationship with us unless and until withdrawn as
described below.

This Consent does not require you to receive every Communication electronically, and we reserve the right, in our sole discretion, to deliver any particular
Communication to you on paper or to require that a given document be signed in ink, notwithstanding your consent. Where we elect to communicate on paper,
we will use the most recent postal address reflected in our records, and such delivery will not affect the continuing validity of your electronic consent for other
Communications.

Electronic Signatures
You agree that your electronic signature on any application, agreement, disclosure acknowledgment, or other document presented to you in electronic form has
the same force and legal effect as a handwritten signature executed with pen and ink. An electronic signature may include, among other methods, clicking an "I
Agree," "Accept," "Sign," or similar button, typing your name into a designated signature field, applying a stylus or finger-drawn mark, or otherwise adopting an
electronic symbol or process with the intent to sign the associated record. You agree not to contest the admissibility, validity, or enforceability of any such
electronically signed record solely on the basis that it was created, signed, or delivered electronically.

When you apply an electronic signature, you represent that you are the person identified in the signature block, that you are authorized to sign in the capacity
indicated, and that you intend to be legally bound by the contents of the document. You further acknowledge that an electronically executed document, together
with any associated audit trail, timestamp, system log, or authentication record maintained by us or our service providers, may be used as evidence of the
existence and terms of your agreement in any administrative or judicial proceeding to the same extent as an original paper document bearing an original
signature.

Hardware and Software Requirements
To access, view, and retain Communications delivered electronically, you must have, at a minimum, a personal computer or mobile device with internet access;
a current and supported web browser capable of supporting industry-standard encryption; software that enables you to view, save, and print documents in
Portable Document Format (PDF) or comparable file formats; sufficient electronic storage capacity or the ability to print and retain copies for your records; and
a valid email account with an active email address that is capable of sending and receiving electronic messages. You are solely responsible for obtaining,
installing, maintaining, and operating the equipment, software, and internet connectivity necessary to receive electronic Communications.

From time to time, the hardware or software required to access, view, or retain Communications may change as technology, security standards, or applicable
law evolve. If our requirements change in a manner that creates a material risk that you will not be able to access or retain a Communication you previously
consented to receive electronically, we will notify you of the revised requirements and afford you the opportunity to withdraw your consent without the
imposition of any fee, condition, or consequence that was not previously disclosed to you. Your continued use of electronic Communications following such
notice constitutes your reaffirmation of this Consent.

Confirming Your Ability to Access
Before you consent to receive Communications electronically, you should confirm that you are able to access information in the electronic form that will be used
to deliver those Communications. You may be asked to demonstrate this ability by viewing this Consent on your device and successfully opening, displaying,
and printing or saving a sample document presented in the same format we will use. By providing your consent electronically, you confirm that your system
meets the requirements described above and that you have been able to access, read, and retain this Consent and any accompanying sample document.

If you do not provide consent, or if you later determine that you are unable to access Communications electronically, you may continue to receive
Communications on paper at no additional charge attributable solely to your election to receive paper, and your decision will not affect the terms of your loan or
your eligibility for any product or service for which you would otherwise qualify.

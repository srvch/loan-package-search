§ 1. Scope of This Document
This document consolidates several distinct borrower notices, certifications, and acknowledgments that ABC Mortgage Company is required or elects to
provide in connection with the origination of the loan referenced above. It is intended to operate together with, and not in substitution for, the promissory note,
security instrument, and all other disclosures delivered to John Doe. Where any provision of this document conflicts with a more specific disclosure mandated
by applicable law, the more specific disclosure shall control to the extent of the conflict, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect
without impairment.

Each section below addresses a separate subject matter and may be relied upon independently. The headings are provided solely for convenience and ease of
reference and do not limit, expand, or otherwise affect the substance of the acknowledgments that follow. John Doe understands that signing this document
does not obligate ABC Mortgage Company to close or fund the loan and does not waive any condition that ABC Mortgage Company may impose prior to
closing. Nothing herein creates any commitment except as expressly stated in a separately executed written commitment from ABC Mortgage Company.

John Doe acknowledges that this document has been made available in a manner permitting review prior to execution, and that John Doe has had a
reasonable opportunity to read it, to ask questions of ABC Mortgage Company, and to seek independent advice from counsel or other advisors of John Doe's
choosing. The execution of this document reflects John Doe's informed and voluntary agreement to the matters described herein as of the date set forth above.

§ 2. Occupancy Certification
John Doe certifies the intended occupancy status of the subject property located at 456 XYZ Avenue, XYZ City, NY 12345 as represented in the loan
application. If the property has been represented as John Doe's principal residence, John Doe certifies an intention to occupy, establish, and use the property
as John Doe's principal residence within the period customarily required following closing, and to continue to occupy the property as a principal residence for
the period required by the loan documents, unless otherwise agreed by ABC Mortgage Company in writing or required by extenuating circumstances beyond
John Doe's reasonable control.

John Doe understands that the occupancy representation is a material factor in ABC Mortgage Company's underwriting decision and in the pricing and terms of
the loan, and that loans secured by a principal residence may carry different terms than loans secured by second homes or investment properties. John Doe
agrees that any change in the intended occupancy of the property prior to closing must be disclosed promptly to ABC Mortgage Company, and acknowledges
that such a change may affect John Doe's eligibility for the loan or the terms upon which it is offered.

John Doe acknowledges that a false certification regarding occupancy may constitute mortgage fraud and may result in acceleration of the loan, civil liability,
and criminal prosecution under applicable law. By executing this document, John Doe affirms that the occupancy status stated in the application is accurate
and reflects John Doe's genuine intention as of the date hereof, and that John Doe will promptly inform ABC Mortgage Company should that intention change
before the loan is consummated.

§ 3. Communicating Intent to Proceed
Following delivery of the Loan Estimate or other early disclosures applicable to the loan, John Doe may indicate an intent to proceed with the transaction. ABC
Mortgage Company will not impose any fee on John Doe in connection with the application, other than a reasonable fee for obtaining John Doe's consumer
report where permitted, until after John Doe has received the applicable early disclosures and has communicated an intent to proceed with the transaction. An
indication of intent to proceed does not obligate John Doe to accept the loan and does not constitute a commitment by ABC Mortgage Company to extend
credit.

John Doe may communicate intent to proceed by any reasonable means, including in person, by telephone at (555) 555-0120, by electronic communication, or
in writing addressed to ABC Mortgage Company at 100 ABC Plaza, XYZ City, NY 12345, in each case in a manner that clearly identifies the loan referenced as
1234567890. Silence or inaction shall not be construed as an indication of intent to proceed. John Doe understands that the interest rate and certain other
terms disclosed in the early disclosures are not locked or guaranteed unless and until a separate rate-lock agreement is executed in accordance with ABC
Mortgage Company's requirements.

John Doe acknowledges that John Doe retains the right to decline to proceed with the transaction at any time prior to consummation, subject to forfeiture only
of fees already incurred and permitted under applicable law. ABC Mortgage Company likewise reserves the right to decline to extend credit if any condition of
approval is not satisfied. Nothing in this section shall be deemed to waive any right of rescission that may apply to the transaction under federal or state law.

§ 4. Anti-Steering Notice
ABC Mortgage Company and any mortgage loan originator acting on its behalf are prohibited by applicable law from steering John Doe toward a loan in order
to increase the originator's compensation, unless the loan is in John Doe's interest. Loan originator compensation may not be based on the terms of the loan
other than the amount of credit extended, and John Doe is entitled to be offered loan options for which John Doe likely qualifies without regard to the
compensation the originator may receive. This notice is provided in general terms and does not itself constitute an offer of any particular loan product.

John Doe acknowledges that John Doe has been or will be presented with information sufficient to make an informed decision among loan options for which
John Doe qualifies, which may include options with the lowest available interest rate, options with the lowest total points and origination fees, and options
without certain risky features, to the extent such options are available and consistent with John Doe's stated objectives. John Doe understands that not all
products are available to every applicant and that eligibility depends upon underwriting and program guidelines applicable to the loan.

Any questions concerning the loan options available to John Doe, the basis on which a particular product was recommended, or the compensation practices
applicable to the transaction may be directed to ABC Mortgage Company at (555) 555-0120. John Doe acknowledges receipt of this anti-steering notice and
affirms that John Doe has had the opportunity to inquire about alternative loan products prior to selecting the loan referenced herein.

We retain the records relating to this transaction, including the loan documents, disclosures, notices, and correspondence, for the periods required by

may involve a research fee.

coverage and to provide evidence of such coverage upon request. Questions regarding flood insurance requirements may be directed to ABC Mortgage
Company at (555) 555-0120.

ABC MORTGAGE COMPANY — NOTICE OF PROPERTY LOCATED IN A SPECIAL FLOOD HAZARD AREA
Loan #1234567890 · ABC Mortgage Company · 12/03/2024
Doc. LN-3329 · Version 3.1 · Effective Date 01/10/2023



This document supersedes any earlier version of the same document furnished to you. Federal law requires ABC Mortgage Company to determine
whether the property offered as security for loan number 1234567890, located at 456 XYZ Avenue, XYZ City, NY 12345, is situated in an area identified
by the Federal Emergency Management Agency as having special flood hazards, and to notify John Doe of that determination. The Standard Flood
Hazard Determination Form completed for this transaction states the result and accompanies this notice or is delivered with your disclosure package.
This document explains what the determination means, when flood insurance is required, how coverage amounts are calculated, and what occurs if
required coverage is not maintained.

1. The Determination
A flood hazard determination identifies the flood zone in which the improvements lie, by reference to the Flood Insurance Rate Map covering the community,
and states whether the community participates in the National Flood Insurance Program. The determination is made by a third-party provider engaged for that
purpose and is documented on the Standard Flood Hazard Determination Form retained in the loan file.

Zones beginning with the letter A or V are special flood hazard areas, in which the requirement to maintain flood insurance applies. Zones designated X, B, or
C are outside the special flood hazard area, and coverage is not federally required, although the risk of flooding is never zero and coverage remains available
and frequently inexpensive in those zones.

2. Credit Reporting
We may furnish information about the status and payment history of loan number 1234567890 to one or more consumer reporting agencies, and we do so in
accordance with the accuracy and dispute-handling requirements that federal law imposes on furnishers. Information furnished ordinarily includes the account
type, the date opened, the balance, the scheduled payment, and the payment status as of the reporting date.

If you believe that information we have furnished about this account is inaccurate or incomplete, you may notify us in writing at 100 ABC Plaza, XYZ City, NY
12345, describing the item and the reason you believe it to be incorrect and enclosing any supporting documentation. We will investigate, report the results to
you, and correct any inaccuracy with each agency to which the information was furnished.

You may also dispute the item directly with the consumer reporting agency, which must generally conduct its own reasonable reinvestigation. Disputing with us
and with the agency are independent routes and may be used together; neither route requires payment of any fee, and no service can lawfully remove
information that is accurate and current.

3. Circumstances Requiring Coverage
Where the improvements securing the loan are located in a special flood hazard area and the community participates in the National Flood Insurance Program,
federal law prohibits the extension of credit unless flood insurance is obtained for the term of the loan. Evidence of coverage must be provided before closing,
and the coverage must remain in force continuously thereafter.

Where the community does not participate in the program, federally backed coverage is unavailable, and the loan may be subject to program restrictions or to
a requirement of private coverage. The consequences of nonparticipation, including the unavailability of federal disaster assistance for certain purposes, are
described in the notice accompanying the determination form.

4. Use of Vendors and Agents
We engage third parties to perform functions in connection with the origination and servicing of residential mortgage loans, including document preparation,
data processing, payment processing, tax and flood determination services, insurance tracking, appraisal management, printing, and mailing. Those providers
receive only the information necessary to perform the service for which they are engaged.

Each provider is bound by contract to maintain the confidentiality and security of the information disclosed to it and to use that information only for the purposes
of the engagement. Engaging a service provider does not relieve us of responsibility for the performance of the function delegated, and inquiries concerning
any such function may be directed to us rather than to the provider.

5. Required Coverage Amounts
The minimum amount of flood insurance required is the least of the outstanding principal balance of the loan, the maximum limit of coverage available for the
property type under the National Flood Insurance Program, or the insurable value of the improvements. Insurable value is generally measured by replacement
cost, or by another method appropriate to the property, and excludes the value of the land.

Contents coverage is not required by the lender and does not satisfy the building-coverage requirement, although you may find it valuable. Where the property
is a unit in a condominium or association covered by a master flood policy, the association's coverage may satisfy part or all of the requirement; evidence of the
master policy and of the allocation applicable to the unit is required.

6. Escrow of Flood Premiums
Federal law requires that premiums for flood insurance be escrowed for most residential loans secured by property in a special flood hazard area, subject to
limited exceptions for small institutions and for certain loan types. Where escrow applies, the premium is collected monthly with your payment and disbursed by
the servicer when the policy renews.

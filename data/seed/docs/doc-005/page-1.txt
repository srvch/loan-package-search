This notice is provided to John Doe by ABC Mortgage Company in connection with loan number 1234567890 and describes the licensing, registration,
and identification requirements applicable to the individuals and entities that originate residential mortgage loans. Federal law requires that every
residential mortgage loan originator be either licensed under the law of the state in which the origination occurs or registered with the Nationwide
Multistate Licensing System and Registry, and that the unique identifier assigned to the originator and to the sponsoring institution appear on specified
loan documents. This notice explains what those identifiers mean, how you may verify them, and what standards of conduct apply to the individual
assisting you as of November 14, 2024. Terms used but not defined in this document have the meanings given to them in your loan documents.

Section 1. Licensing and Registration Requirement
The Secure and Fair Enforcement for Mortgage Licensing Act establishes a national framework for the supervision of residential mortgage loan originators. An
individual who takes a residential mortgage loan application, or who offers or negotiates the terms of a residential mortgage loan for compensation or gain,
must be licensed by the appropriate NY regulator or, if employed by a federally insured depository institution or its subsidiary, registered with the Registry.
Licensing entails testing, pre-licensure and continuing education, a criminal-background review, a credit review, and the posting of a surety bond or its
equivalent.

Both the sponsoring institution and the individual originator are assigned a unique identifier by the Registry. That identifier stays with the individual across
employers and across states, which allows a consumer to trace an originator's employment and disciplinary history over the course of a career. ABC Mortgage
Company maintains the licensure and registration status of each individual authorized to originate loans on its behalf and does not permit an unlicensed or
unregistered person to perform origination activity.

Section 2. Where the Identifiers Appear
The unique identifier of the individual originator who assisted you and that of ABC Mortgage Company appear on your loan application, on the Loan Estimate
and Closing Disclosure, and on the note, in the fields designated for that purpose. The identifiers are also included in advertising, on business cards, and in
electronic correspondence relating to your transaction. If you receive a document relating to loan number 1234567890 that omits an identifier where one is
required, you may request that a corrected copy be furnished to you.

The identifier is a licensing reference number only. It is not an account number, it conveys no authority to access your loan, and it does not by itself indicate that
any particular individual is authorized to receive payment from you. Payments and payoff funds should be directed only to the addresses and accounts
identified in your payment coupon, billing statement, or written payoff statement.

Section 3. Standards of Conduct
A licensed or registered originator is prohibited from employing any scheme or device to defraud or mislead a borrower, from engaging in unfair or deceptive
practices, from obtaining property by fraud or misrepresentation, and from soliciting or entering into a contract in which the compensation is contingent upon a
specified appraised value. An originator may not make, in any manner, a false or deceptive statement concerning the rates, points, or other financing terms
available to a consumer.

Originators are further prohibited from negligently making a false statement or knowingly omitting a material fact in connection with information provided to a
federal or state regulator. ABC Mortgage Company maintains written policies, periodic training, and supervisory review designed to ensure that the individuals
acting on its behalf observe these standards throughout the origination of loan number 1234567890.

Section 4. Transfer of the Loan
We may sell, assign, transfer, pledge, or otherwise convey the loan, the loan documents, or the right to service the loan, in whole or in part, at any time and
without your consent, except as applicable law requires. A transfer of ownership or of servicing does not by itself change the interest rate, the payment amount,
the maturity date, or any other term of your obligation, and the transferee succeeds to the rights and, to the extent of the transfer, the obligations described in
the loan documents.

This document binds and benefits you and us and our respective heirs, executors, administrators, successors, and permitted assigns. You may not assign or
delegate your rights or obligations without our prior written consent, and the sale or transfer of the property at 456 XYZ Avenue, XYZ City, NY 12345 does not
release you from liability for the debt unless we expressly agree in writing to a release.

Section 5. Loan Originator Compensation
Federal law prohibits compensating a loan originator on the basis of the terms of a transaction, including the interest rate, the presence or absence of a
prepayment penalty, or any proxy for such a term. Compensation may be based on the amount of credit extended, on the number of transactions closed, on
the quality of the files produced, or on similar permissible factors. This restriction is designed to remove any financial incentive for an originator to steer you
toward terms less favorable than those for which you qualify.

An originator may not receive compensation from both you and another person in connection with the same transaction. Where you pay the originator directly,
no compensation may be received from the creditor or any other party, and where the creditor pays, no additional origination compensation may be collected
from you. The compensation arrangement applicable to your transaction is reflected in the origination charges disclosed on your Loan Estimate and Closing
Disclosure.

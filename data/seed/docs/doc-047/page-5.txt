                                                    SCHEDULE B, PART I: Requirements
All of the following Requirements must be met before the Policy or Policies described in Schedule A may be issued:
   No.   Requirement

     1 Pay the agreed amount for the interest in the Land and/or the Mortgage to be insured.
     2 Pay the premiums, fees and charges for the Policy to the Company in accordance with the Company's filed rate schedule.
     3 The Company must receive documents satisfactory to it that convey the Title or create the Mortgage to be insured, or both,
       properly authorized, executed, delivered and recorded in the Public Records:
           (a) General Warranty Deed from Sam Seller and Sue Seller, the vested owner, to John Doe and Jane Doe, conveying the
         Land described in Schedule A.
           (b) Mortgage / Deed of Trust from John Doe and Jane Doe to ABC Mortgage Company securing the principal sum of
         $496,000.00.
     4 Satisfaction, release or payoff of record of the mortgage of record given by the vested owner, together with the recorded
       release of any assignment thereof.
     5 Payment of all taxes, assessments and municipal charges levied against the Land that are due and payable as of the date of
       closing, together with any penalty or interest.
     6 Owner's Affidavit and Indemnity (including gap, mechanic's lien and parties-in-possession coverage) executed by the
       vested owner and delivered to the Company at or before closing.
     7 Proof satisfactory to the Company of the identity of all parties to the transaction, as required by applicable federal law.
     8 Receipt by the Company of a current survey of the Land, certified to the Company and to the Proposed Insured, in order to
       delete the survey exception from the Policy.
     9 Receipt by the Company of the fully executed settlement statement for the transaction, approved by the lender and by all
       parties.
    10 Written payoff authorization for each lien to be released, valid through the disbursement date.
    11 Evidence satisfactory to the Company that all homeowners association assessments and transfer charges affecting the Land
       are paid current through the date of closing.
    12 This Commitment is subject to the Company's continued examination of title through the date of recording; matters
       appearing of record between the Commitment Date and recording will be added as exceptions unless disposed of to the
       Company's satisfaction.

If any Requirement is not satisfied, the Company may, at its option, decline to issue the Policy, add exceptions to the Policy, or amend this Commitment.
Satisfaction of a Requirement does not waive any Exception set forth in Schedule B, Part II unless the Company has expressly agreed in writing to delete
that Exception.

                                                        NOTES TO SCHEDULE B, PART I
 NOTE 1. Wire fraud advisory. Wire instructions issued by XYZ Title Company will not change during the course of this
         transaction. Any communication purporting to amend wire instructions must be verified by telephone with the
         closing office at (555) 555-0130 using a number independently obtained before any funds are transmitted.
 NOTE 2. Closing protection. A closing protection letter will be issued to ABC Mortgage Company upon request and prior
         to disbursement, covering the acts of the Policy Issuing Agent in connection with the closing of the insured
         transaction.
 NOTE 3. Recording and disbursement. All documents identified in Requirement 3 must be delivered to the Company for
         recording in the Public Records of XYZ County, NY. Recording fees, documentary stamps and transfer taxes are
         payable at closing in accordance with the settlement statement and are not insured against by the Policy.

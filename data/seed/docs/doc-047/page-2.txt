 5.        LIMITATIONS OF LIABILITY. The Company’s liability is limited to the Proposed Insured’s actual expense
           incurred in the interval between the Proposed Insured’s delivery to the Company of a request for the Policy and the
           Company’s delivery of the amended Commitment, resulting from the Proposed Insured’s good faith reliance on this
           Commitment. The Company is not liable for the Proposed Insured’s negotiation of any agreement, or for the Proposed
           Insured’s expenditures for improvements to the Land.
 6.        LIABILITY OF THE COMPANY MUST BE BASED ON THIS COMMITMENT. Only a Proposed Insured
           identified in Schedule A may make a claim under this Commitment, and any claim must be based in contract under
           the terms of this Commitment and is restricted to the terms and provisions of this Commitment.
 7.        PRO-FORMA POLICY. The Company may provide, at the request of a Proposed Insured, a pro-forma policy
           illustrating the coverage the Company may provide. A pro-forma policy neither reflects the status of Title at the date it
           is prepared or delivered, nor is it a commitment to insure.
 8.        CLAIMS PROCEDURES. This Commitment incorporates by reference all Conditions for making a claim in the
           Policy to be issued to the Proposed Insured.
 9.        CLASS ACTION. All claims and disputes arising out of or relating to this Commitment shall be brought in an
           individual capacity, and no party may serve as a class member in any purported class or representative proceeding.

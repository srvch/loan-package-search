Where the property is located in a special flood hazard area, flood insurance is required by federal law for the term of the loan, in an amount at least equal to
the lesser of the outstanding principal balance, the insurable value of the improvements, or the maximum available under the National Flood Insurance
Program. The determination applicable to your property is disclosed to you separately.

master policy. Review the association's coverage where applicable to confirm what it does and does not insure.

Article XII. Handling of Loss Proceeds
Report a loss to your carrier promptly and notify the servicer, because the security instrument gives the lender an interest in insurance proceeds. Where a loss
is significant, proceeds are commonly issued jointly to you and to the servicer and are disbursed against the progress of repairs under a loss-draft process that
requires documentation such as the adjuster's report, contractor estimates, and inspections.

The purpose of that process is to ensure that proceeds are used to restore the collateral. It is administered under the terms of the security instrument and
applicable law. Contact the servicer as soon as a loss occurs so that the requirements are understood before repairs begin and so that disbursements are not
delayed by missing documentation.

        ABC MORTGAGE COMPANY — INTEREST RATE LOCK-IN POLICY
        Loan #1234567890 : ABC Mortgage Company « 12/02/2024
        Form MTG-2096 : Rev. 4.4 - Effective 12/09/2023
        Where this document summarizes the law, the law itself controls in the event of any difference. This document describes the policy of ABC Mortgage
         Company concerning interest rate lock-in agreements for residential mortgage transactions, including the application identified as loan number
         4234567890 for the property at 456 XYZ Avenue, XYZ City, NY 12345. A rate lock is a commitment, for a stated period and subject to stated conditions,
         to make credit available to John Doe at a specified interest rate and point combination. The specific rate, points, lock period, and expiration date
         applicable to your transaction, if a lock has been established, appear on the separate rate lock confirmation issued to you; this policy explains the rules
         that govern locks generally.
         1) Choosing to Lock
         Until a lock is established, the rate applicable to your transaction floats, meaning that the rate available to you moves with the market and is not determined
         until a lock is taken or, at the fatest, until the point at which the loan documents are prepared. A floating rate exposes you to the possibility of both improvement
         and deterioration in pricing before closing.
         Locking fixes the rate and points for the lock period, transferring the risk of market movement during that period to the lender, which manages the exposure
         through its own hedging arrangements. Neither choice is inherently better; the decision depends on your tolerance for uncertainty, the time remaining before
         closing, and the state of the market when the decision is made.
         2) How a Lock Is Taken
         A lock is established only when we confirm it in writing. An oral discussion of available pricing, a rate quoted in a marketing communication, a rate shown on an
         initial Loan Estimate issued before a lock, and a rate discussed in general conversation are not locks and create no commitment. If you have not received a
         written lock confirmation identifying the rate, the points, the lock period, and the expiration date, your rate is floating.
         Establishing a lock generally requires a complete application, a property address, and a loan program selection, because pricing depends on program,
         occupancy, property type, loan amount, credit score, and loan-to-value ratio. A change in any of those characteristics after a lock is taken may require the lock
         to be repriced under the terms described below.
         3) Reservation of Rights
          No failure or delay on our part in exercising any right, power, or privilege under the loan documents operates as a waiver of it, and no single or partial exercise
         of any right precludes a further exercise of that right or the exercise of any other. Any waiver must be in writing and signed by an authorized officer of ABC
          Mortgage Company to be effective, and it extends only to the specific matter identified in it.
          Our rights and remedies are cumulative and may be exercised concurrently, successively, or in any order. Nothing in this document creates any right in favor of
          a third party, and no course of dealing between you and us, and no accommodation extended on one occasion, varies the written terms of the loan documents
          or establishes a practice on which either party may rely thereafter.
          Correspondingly, nothing in this document waives, limits, or diminishes any right, protection, or remedy available to you under federal law or under the law of
          NY. To the extent any provision of this document is inconsistent with such a right, the right controls and the remaining provisions continue in effect.
          4) Translation and Language Access
          This document is provided in English. If English is not your primary language, you may wish to have it reviewed with the assistance of a translator or an adviser
          of your choosing before you rely upon it. Where we make a translated version of a document available, the translation is furnished as a courtesy; unless
          applicable law provides otherwise, the English version governs the rights and obligations of the parties.
          Free language assistance is available through the housing counseling organizations identified in the notice furnished to you concerning the availability of
          counseling, many of which offer services in multiple languages. If you require assistance in communicating with us about loan number 1234567890, call (555)
          555-0120 and identify the language you prefer so that we may make appropriate arrangements.
          5) Effect on Your Loan Documents
          This document supplements the note, the security instrument, and the disclosures furnished to you in connection with loan number 1234567890. It does not
          enlarge, reduce, or otherwise modify the amounts payable, the interest rate, the payment schedule, or any right or remedy established by those instruments,
          and no statement here should be read as a representation that any particular term applies to your transaction.
          Where this document summarizes a statute, a regulation, or a program requirement, the summary is provided for convenience. In the event of any difference
          between the summary and the authority summarized, the statute, regulation, or program requirement controls, and no summary in this document limits any
          right afforded to you by law.
           6) Lock Periods and Expiration
           Locks are offered for defined periods, commonly thirty, forty-five, or sixty days, and longer periods are available for certain programs at a price that reflects the
           additional exposure. The lock expires at the close of business on the expiration date shown on the confirmation. A lock must be valid on the date the loan is
           funded, not merely on the date the documents are signed.

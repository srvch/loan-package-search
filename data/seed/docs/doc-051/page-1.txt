(c) What a Notice of Error Must Contain
A qualifying notice of error must state the name of the borrower, information that enables the servicer to identify the account, and the error asserted. It need not
be submitted on any particular form and need not use the words "notice of error," but it must describe the error with enough specificity that the servicer can
investigate the matter. A general expression of dissatisfaction is not a notice of error.

Covered errors include failing to accept a payment that conforms to the servicer's written requirements, failing to apply an accepted payment properly, failing to
credit a payment as of the date of receipt, failing to make a timely escrow disbursement, imposing a fee or charge without a reasonable basis, failing to provide
an accurate payoff balance, and any other error relating to the servicing of the loan.

(d) What a Request for Information Must Contain
A request for information must state the name of the borrower, information that enables the servicer to identify the account, and the information sought. A
request may seek documents such as a payment history, an escrow analysis, a copy of the note, or the identity of the owner or assignee of the loan. A request
seeking the identity of the owner or assignee is subject to a shorter response deadline than other requests.

A servicer is not required to respond to a request that is overbroad or unduly burdensome, to one that seeks confidential, proprietary, or privileged information,
to one that is irrelevant to the servicing of the loan, or to one that is duplicative of a request already answered. Where a response is declined on one of those
grounds, the servicer notifies you in writing and states the basis.

Appraisal and Written Valuation Delivery Disclosure
IMPORTANT NOTICE — APPRAISAL AND WRITTEN VALUATION DELIVERY DISCLOSURE
Loan #1234567890 · ABC Mortgage Company · 12/05/2024
Document LN-5795 · Rev. 1.5 · In effect as of 09/01/2023



This notice is furnished by ABC Mortgage Company to John Doe in connection with loan number 1234567890 and the application for credit to be
secured by a first lien on the residential property located at 456 XYZ Avenue, XYZ City, NY 12345. Federal law entitles you to receive a copy of each
appraisal and each other written valuation developed in connection with your application, promptly upon completion and in any event no later than the
applicable regulatory deadline before consummation. This notice describes how and when those copies will be delivered, the fees that may be charged
for the underlying valuation work, and the steps you may take if you believe a valuation contains a factual error or omits information material to the
opinion of value. If any part of this document is unclear to you, contact us before you sign or rely upon it.

1. Right to Copies of Valuations
We may order an appraisal to determine the value of the property identified above and will charge you for that appraisal in accordance with the fee disclosures
provided to you separately. We will promptly give you a copy of any appraisal, even if your loan does not close, and we will do so whether or not the appraisal
supports the value we are asked to finance. A written valuation for this purpose includes an appraisal report prepared by a licensed or certified appraiser, an
automated valuation model report, a broker price opinion, and any other written estimate of the market value of the property that we develop or obtain in
connection with your application.

You may pay for an additional appraisal for your own use, obtained from an appraiser of your own choosing and at your own expense, and nothing in this
notice restricts that right. Any appraisal you obtain independently is your property, is not part of our credit file, and will not by itself alter the value conclusion
upon which our credit decision rests. If you elect to submit such a report to us for consideration, we will review it under the same standards applied to any other
valuation evidence submitted in support of an application.

2. Third-Party Service Providers
We engage third parties to perform functions in connection with the origination and servicing of residential mortgage loans, including document preparation,
data processing, payment processing, tax and flood determination services, insurance tracking, appraisal management, printing, and mailing. Those providers
receive only the information necessary to perform the service for which they are engaged.

Each provider is bound by contract to maintain the confidentiality and security of the information disclosed to it and to use that information only for the purposes
of the engagement. Engaging a service provider does not relieve us of responsibility for the performance of the function delegated, and inquiries concerning
any such function may be directed to us rather than to the provider.

3. Delivery Deadlines
We will deliver a copy of each written valuation promptly upon completion, or no later than three (3) business days before consummation of the transaction,
whichever is earlier. Where the credit is denied, the application is withdrawn, or the transaction is otherwise not consummated, copies will be delivered
promptly and in no event later than thirty (30) days after we determine that the transaction will not close. Delivery is measured from the date we receive the
completed valuation from the appraiser or valuation provider, not from the date the report is signed or reviewed internally.

You may waive the requirement that copies be delivered three business days before consummation, provided the waiver is given at least three business days
before that date. If you waive the advance-delivery timing, we will still deliver all copies at or before consummation, and no waiver relieves us of the obligation
to deliver copies without charge. A waiver granted in connection with loan number 1234567890 applies only to the timing of delivery and never to your
underlying right to receive the documents themselves.

4. Method of Delivery
Copies will be delivered by the method you have elected for receipt of loan communications. If you have consented to electronic delivery, we will transmit each
valuation to the electronic address on file or post it to the secure document portal associated with your application and notify you that it is available. If you have
not consented to electronic delivery, we will mail copies by first-class mail, postage prepaid, to your address of record. Delivery is complete when the document
is mailed or transmitted, regardless of when it is opened or retrieved.

If a valuation is revised, supplemented, or replaced after an initial copy has been delivered to you, we will deliver the revised report under the same timing rules
that governed the original. Multiple valuations obtained for a single property are each delivered separately; we do not substitute a summary, a review report, or
an internal analysis for the underlying valuation document. You may request an additional copy at any time during the life of the application by contacting us at
(555) 555-0120.

5. Fees for Appraisal Services
We may charge you a reasonable fee for the cost of the appraisal or other valuation obtained in connection with your application, and the amount and payee of
that fee are disclosed on your Loan Estimate and Closing Disclosure rather than in this notice. We may not charge any fee for furnishing the copies to which
this notice entitles you. Where a valuation is performed by a third-party vendor or appraisal management company, the fee reflects the amount charged to us
for that service, and the composition of the fee is disclosed to you as required by applicable law.

If your application is withdrawn or denied after a valuation has been ordered, the fee for work already performed remains payable in accordance with the
authorization you provided at the time the order was placed. We will refund any amount collected for services that were not performed. Questions concerning
appraisal fees, the identity of the appraisal management company engaged, or the timing of any refund may be directed to ABC Mortgage Company at 100
ABC Plaza, XYZ City, NY 12345.

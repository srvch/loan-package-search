Article I. What Constitutes a Full Payment
A full contractual payment consists of the scheduled principal and interest, plus the monthly escrow deposit where an escrow account is maintained, plus any
other recurring amount identified on your periodic statement, such as an optional product premium you have elected. The total appears on your billing
statement as the amount due. Any remittance below that total is a partial payment regardless of how close it comes to the amount due.

Amounts past due from a prior period are added to the current amount due. Where more than one payment is outstanding, the total due reflects each unpaid
installment together with any late charge properly assessed, and a remittance sufficient to cover only one installment is applied to the oldest outstanding
installment first.

Where this document summarizes a statute, a regulation, or a program requirement, the summary is provided for convenience. In the event of any difference

Article III. Order in Which Payments Are Applied
Unless applicable law or the terms of your note require otherwise, a payment received and accepted is applied first to accrued interest, then to principal, then
to the escrow deposit, then to any late charges outstanding, and finally to other fees and advances properly assessed against the account. This is the order
used for every regular monthly payment and is not varied at the election of the servicer.

The order of application governs the composition of the payment, not whether the payment satisfies the obligation. A payment applied in this order still leaves
the installment unpaid if the total remitted is less than the amount due, and the account is reported as it actually stands after application.

Article IV. Partial Payments and Suspense
Where the servicer accepts a partial payment, the funds are ordinarily placed in a suspense or unapplied-funds account rather than applied immediately to the
loan. Funds remain in suspense until enough has accumulated to make a full contractual payment, at which point the full amount is applied in the standard
order and credited as of the date the account reached that total.

Funds held in suspense do not reduce the principal balance and do not stop the accrual of interest. Your periodic statement identifies any amount held in
suspense so that the status of the account is visible to you. If you prefer that a partial remittance be returned rather than held, notify the servicer at (555)
555-0120 at the time the payment is made.

Article V. Successors and Assigns
We may sell, assign, transfer, pledge, or otherwise convey the loan, the loan documents, or the right to service the loan, in whole or in part, at any time and
without your consent, except as applicable law requires. A transfer of ownership or of servicing does not by itself change the interest rate, the payment amount,
the maturity date, or any other term of your obligation, and the transferee succeeds to the rights and, to the extent of the transfer, the obligations described in
the loan documents.

This document binds and benefits you and us and our respective heirs, executors, administrators, successors, and permitted assigns. You may not assign or
delegate your rights or obligations without our prior written consent, and the sale or transfer of the property at 456 XYZ Avenue, XYZ City, NY 12345 does not
release you from liability for the debt unless we expressly agree in writing to a release.

Article VI. Extra Principal Payments
You may pay more than the amount due at any time. To ensure that the extra amount is applied to principal rather than held for the next installment, indicate
the additional principal amount on the coupon or in the designated field of the online payment screen, or include a written instruction identifying the amount and
referencing loan number 1234567890. An unmarked overpayment may be held toward the next scheduled installment.

An additional principal payment reduces the outstanding balance and the total interest paid over the life of the loan, but it does not change the amount or the
due date of the next scheduled payment unless your note expressly provides otherwise. Continue to make each regular monthly payment in full after making
an additional principal payment.
